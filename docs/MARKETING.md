# Potify ID marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Independent creators and platform engineers. **Offer.** Let someone carry credit for a contribution from the Greenhouse to a creator screening.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Demonstrate a captioner's crew badge unlocking a screening while the guest list contains no wallet address. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Run the two-app example. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 2 external developers reproduce the fixture. |

### Sales test

Offer a creator a free technical walkthrough. A future paid onboarding agreement must cover the organizer integration and a witnessed, consented event check-in. Seek one paid onboarding engagement only after an organizer confirms the integration and a live acceptance test passes.

### Measurement and decision

Use `id-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** Local two-app tests and actual website branch source are verified. A deployed issuer, recovery on iPhone and independent partner adoption remain outstanding. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
